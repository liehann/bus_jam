# Campfire kit: one piece per Gemini chat

Attach `art/raw/camping_night/mock.jpeg` (the Campfire level mock, image 1) to every prompt below, one new chat per piece,
and save each result as `art/raw/camping_night/<piece>.<ext>` (the name after "Save as"). Then run
`python3 art/tools/theme_kit.py camping_night`. What each piece is for: docs/ART-PIPELINE.md, "Level themes".

Using exactly its art style (flat 2D illustration, soft cel shading, thick dark brown outlines, warm woodland palette with deep blue night accents, warm soft firelight and lantern glow at night), paint the empty background scene of image 1 for a tall portrait phone screen, 9:20 aspect ratio: the same scene as image 1, a cozy forest campsite at night for cute animals, seen from directly above, with every game piece removed. No conveyor belts, no loop or anything inside it, no plates, no kitchen, no counter, no benches, no animals, no board, no mat, no cards, no buttons, no text. The whole middle of the image is one calm, even, plain dark blue-green forest floor with a few soft warm firelight pools, nothing on it, because the game draws its pieces there. All decoration sits in a thin border along the edges only: a few small pale birch logs, ferns and pine branches tucked into the four corners only, a couple of fireflies; no sky, no stars and no wide border. Flat top-down view, no perspective.
